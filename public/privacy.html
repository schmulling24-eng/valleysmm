Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Valley SMM Kenya

Valley SMM ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number (for M-Pesa).
  • Transaction data: Deposit amounts, M-Pesa codes, order history.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, IP address, browser type.
  • Order data: Links you submit for service delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process payments and wallet transactions.
  • To deliver the SMM services you order.
  • To send transactional emails (order updates, receipts).
  • To improve our platform and prevent fraud.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. M-Pesa Data

When you make a payment via M-Pesa, your phone number and transaction code are stored to confirm and credit your deposit. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN — PIN entry happens directly on your phone via Safaricom's secure STK Push.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share limited data with:

  • Safaricom: For M-Pesa payment processing.
  • Upstream SMM providers: Your order link and quantity to fulfill orders (no personal info shared).
  • Law enforcement: If required by Kenyan law.

5. Data Security

Your password is stored as a bcrypt hash and never in plain text. All API calls use JWT tokens with expiry. We use HTTPS encryption for all communications. Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Cookies

We use localStorage (not cookies) to store your session token on your browser. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Transaction records are kept for 7 years for financial compliance. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@valleysmm.co.ke.

9. Children's Privacy

Valley SMM is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@valleysmm.co.ke.
